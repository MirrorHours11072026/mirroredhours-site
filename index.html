Mirrored Hours

A co-op paranormal investigation game by AE Media

About the game

Mirrored Hours is a first-person horror game for up to four players. You and your team take jobs at haunted places around a small, strange town after dark. Search the building, gather evidence with your equipment, and work out which kind of ghost you are dealing with before it works out where you are.

Every ghost behaves differently, and the clues only mean something if you read them correctly. Stay close to your team, watch your sanity, and get out with what you need.

What you do

  • Investigate haunted locations as a team of up to four.
  • Use tools such as EMF readers, thermal cameras, spirit boxes and recorders to find evidence.
  • Identify the ghost, then report back to get paid.
  • Earn money and experience to unlock new gear, places and harder difficulties.

Mirrored Hours is developed and published by AE Media. It is coming to the Epic Games Store.
