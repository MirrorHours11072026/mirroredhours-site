Privacy Policy

AE Media · Mirrored Hours · Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Mirrored Hours and this website (mirroredhours.com) capture, use, share and keep information. AE Media is the developer and publisher. The short version: we collect very little, we use it only to run the game, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

1. What information is captured

  • Account identifiers. If you sign in with an Epic Games or Steam account to play online, the game receives your account ID and display name.
  • Game progress. Your money, experience, unlocks, owned equipment, settings and similar progress are saved in a profile file on your own computer.
  • Voice chat. In online games, your microphone audio is captured while you talk and sent to the other players in your session. Voice chat only happens in multiplayer, and you can mute your microphone.
  • Technical data. To run matchmaking and multiplayer, Epic Online Services processes connection details such as your IP address.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.
  • Website. This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. Its host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type).

2. How the information is used

  • Account identifiers: to sign you in, connect you to other players and match you to your saved progress.
  • Game progress: to load and save your game, and, when you sign in with Epic, to keep your progress when you change computers.
  • Voice chat: only to let you talk to the players in your session.
  • Technical data: only to run multiplayer connections and keep the service working.
  • Emails to us: only to answer you and to look into problems you report.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or marketing.

3. How the information is shared

  • Other players. In an online game, other players in your session can see your display name and hear your voice.
  • Epic Games and Steam. Online sign-in, matchmaking and, for Epic accounts, the cloud copy of your progress are handled by Epic Online Services (and Steam where you use it). They process that information under their own privacy policies.
  • Service providers. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. How long the information is kept

  • Local profile. Stays on your computer until you delete the game's save folder.
  • Epic cloud copy. If you sign in with Epic, a copy of your progress is stored with Epic Online Services until you ask for it to be deleted or you delete it through your Epic account.
  • Voice chat. Not recorded and not stored by us. It is heard live and then gone.
  • Technical data. Held by Epic Online Services (or Steam) under their retention rules; we do not keep a copy.
  • Emails to us. Kept only as long as needed to answer you and resolve the issue, then deleted.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your local game profile at any time by removing the game's save folder, and you can mute your microphone in game. To ask what data we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it (including the Epic cloud copy), contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
