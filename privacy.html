Privacy Policy

AE Media · Mirrored Hours · Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Mirrored Hours and this website (mirroredhours.com) use, and why. AE Media is the developer and publisher.

Information the game uses

  • Account identifiers. If you sign in with an Epic Games or Steam account to play online, the game receives your account ID and display name so we can connect you to other players and save your progress.
  • Game progress. Your money, experience, unlocks, settings and similar progress are saved in a profile on your computer. When you sign in with Epic, a copy may be stored with Epic Online Services so your progress follows you.
  • Voice chat. In online games, your microphone audio is sent to the other players in your session so you can talk. It is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Technical data. Epic Online Services may process connection details such as your IP address to run matchmaking and multiplayer.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising trackers in the game or on this website.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Third-party services

Online features run on Epic Online Services and, where used, Steam. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they handle. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

You can delete your local game profile at any time by removing the game's save folder. To ask about, correct or delete data we hold about you, contact us at the address below.
